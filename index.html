            How it works

            Up and running in three steps.

            1. STEP 01

              Add your products

              Scan them in one by one, or import your wholesaler's .csv list in one go. Set till keys for loose items.

            2. STEP 02

              Sell at the counter

              Scan or press a till key, then complete the sale. The receipt prints on your thermal printer.

            3. STEP 03

              Pair your iPhone

              Scan the pairing code in the till's Settings with your iPhone camera. The app connects over the shop Wi-Fi.

            Questions

            Before you open up.

            What do I need at the counter?

            A Windows PC to run the till, a USB barcode scanner that types like a keyboard, and a thermal receipt printer for 58mm or 80mm paper. The iPhone app connects to the till over the shop Wi-Fi.

            Does the app need the internet?

            No. The app never contacts the internet. It only talks to your till over the shop network, so your sales data stays in the shop.

            Can I import my product list?

            Yes. Load a .csv file from your wholesaler. Only the product name is required, and columns like "Product Name", "RRP" or "Case Size" are matched for you.

            Can staff use the till without seeing the takings?

            Yes. Give them a Cashier login and they see the till only. Administrators also see the Dashboard, Inventory, End of Day and staff settings.

            How does the end of day work?

            End of Day shows net takings, card and cash totals, the cash drawer, VAT, best sellers and every receipt. Print an X read during the day, then close the day when you shut.

            When can I get the iPhone app?

            EPOS Companion is coming to the App Store soon.
